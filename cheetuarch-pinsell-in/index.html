Architecture Across Punjab

Designing Modern Homes, Luxury Kothis & Commercial Spaces Across Malout and Sri Muktsar Sahib

Cheetu Architects is a trusted architecture and home design studio serving Malout, Sri Muktsar Sahib, Abohar, Bathinda, Gidderbaha, Fazilka and surrounding regions of Punjab. Our work ranges from modern kothi front elevation design and luxury villa architecture to commercial buildings, interior design and complete construction planning.

Whether you are searching for the best architects in Malout Punjab, experienced home designers in Sri Muktsar Sahib, a vastu-compliant luxury home architect, or professional guidance for a double storey kothi, our team combines decades of engineering experience with contemporary architectural design.

Every project is tailored to Punjab's climate, lifestyle and family needs. We focus on natural ventilation, energy-efficient planning, durable facade materials and timeless aesthetics that remain beautiful for generations.

Vastu Architecture

Vastu-Compliant Luxury Homes Designed For Everyday Living

Many homeowners want the reassurance of vastu principles without sacrificing contemporary architecture.

At Cheetu Architects, vastu is integrated thoughtfully into the planning process — considering entrances, circulation, room placement, natural light and spatial harmony.

The result is a home that feels balanced, practical and aligned with both tradition and modern family life.
